### Std 5 rebuild resolves the "missing OR questions"

The user reported two questions missing from Std 5's OR section. Rather than patch the old OR structure, the new spec removes OR from Std 5 entirely and replaces it with a fixed A(6)+B(3)+C(3)+D(4)+E(4) layout — six MCQs, three fill-ins, three true/false, a four-row match, and four short-answer questions. This matches the authoritative spec verbatim, so the fix is by supersession: there is no longer an OR section that could be missing questions. The same restructure applied to Std 6–8; Std 9–10 gained a section F application/AI question worth 3 marks.
