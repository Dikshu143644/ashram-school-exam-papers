# Rebalance every class paper to exactly 20 marks (v1)

Every exam paper in `index.html` was trimmed so its sections sum to exactly 20 marks. Std 1–4 were already at 20 and were left untouched; Std 5–8 (previously 30) and Std 9–10 (previously 40) were reduced by deleting whole question items and match rows, lowering each `sec-marks` label, renumbering `Q.N` sequentially, and updating the meta-chip total. The nav `tab-subtext` labels and the syllabus marks column in `Computer_Std_1_to_10_Question_Papers.md` were updated to match. The reduction removed complete items rather than editing surviving MCQs, so the shuffled correct-answer positions merged in PR #2 stay intact, and the descriptive/theory sections required for Std 8, 9, and 10 were preserved.

Watch for: nothing blocking. The one thing worth a glance is that the time allowances for Std 8/9/10 (75–90 min) were left unchanged and now look generous for a 20-mark paper — a content judgment, not a correctness issue *(possible)*.

**Verdict**: APPROVED

## High-level view

The rebalancing hit its numeric target cleanly. All six edited papers (Std 5–10) sum to exactly 20, each `sec-marks` label equals the real count of gradable items in its section (match sections counted by rows, विभाग F by the explicit `[N गुण]` tags), `Q.N` runs 1–16 (Std 5–8) or 1–14 (Std 9–10) with no gaps or duplicates, and every meta-chip reads `एकूण गुण: २० (Max Marks: 20)`. No stray 30/40-mark labels remain anywhere.

The shuffle from PR #2 survived. Because the trim deleted entire `q-item` blocks and the last match rows rather than touching surviving questions, every MCQ kept from the base branch has byte-identical option ordering — verified by diffing question-to-option maps against `main`. Nothing was reordered or reset to position A.

The theory requirement for the upper classes is satisfied. Std 8 keeps विभाग E as four short-answer write-line questions; Std 9 and Std 10 keep both विभाग E (short-answer) and विभाग F (application) with real write-line marks, so none of the three was collapsed into a pure-objective paper.

Scope was respected on both sides. Std 1–4 articles are byte-identical to `main`, and the bundled markdown change is confined to the syllabus table's marks column; the full papers in that file only go up to Std 2, so no stale full-paper content was left behind.
